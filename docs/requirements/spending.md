# Spending (`CASH`)

Spending shows what actually came in and went out over a calendar period, or in an average month over the past 3, 6 or 12 months, worked out from transactions: total income, expenses and savings, what was left over, and the savings rate, with a breakdown by category or by category group, laid out as the Budget is. It's the other side of the Budget (BUD): the budget is the plan, Spending is what happened.

Screen: **Spending** (`/spending`). It was called Cashflow until 2026-09-28; `/cashflow` still redirects here, and its requirements keep the `CASH` prefix.

## CASH-1 The user can see where their money came from and went

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| CASH-1.1 | The user can choose a month, a quarter or a year to view. A row between the summary card and the Income section shall hold, on one line down to phone widths, a **Month** / **Quarter** / **Year** / **Average** switch (Average is CASH-1.12) and the period ("May 2026", "Q2 2026" or "2026"; on phones the month is shortened, "Sep 2026"), as outlined pills of the same height with their text centred. The period is sized for its longest value, so it doesn't change width as the choices change, and on phones the two share the row's whole width. Choosing another kind in the switch shows the period of that kind that holds today when the one on screen does, and otherwise the one its first day falls in (May 2026 becomes Q2 2026; 2025 becomes January 2025). Selecting the period opens a picker, a popover below it on wider screens and a bottom sheet on phones, with a grid of the months, quarters or years to choose from, the one on screen marked, and for months and quarters a year stepper. The years on offer run from the oldest transaction's year to the newest's, and always include the current year. Choosing one shows it and closes the picker; Escape or clicking outside closes it without a change. Spending shall open on the current month. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.2 | The figures shall be built from the sum of transaction amounts per category for every transaction dated within the chosen period (both ends included). Transactions in a hidden account (ACCT-1.9), and hidden transactions (TXN-1.8), shall be left out. Transactions in closed accounts (ACCT-6) count. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.3 | Amounts in categories of Income groups shall count as **income**, and amounts in categories of Expense groups as **expenses**. A category of a Transfer group shall count as **savings** by the money it moved out of everyday accounts (Checking, Other Cash and Credit Card, ACCT-1.2) less the money it moved back into them, so moving $500 from checking to savings saves $500, bringing $100 back takes $100 off, and a credit card payment or a move between two savings accounts saves nothing and is left out. Uncategorized amounts (CAT-5) shall count by sign: money in as income, money out as expenses. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.4 | A summary card shall lead with **Left over**: income minus expenses minus savings in whole dollars, green when zero or more, red with a "-" when negative ("-$3,734"), as Budget's does (BUD-1.10). Below, **Income** (green), **Expenses** (as a positive number, in the ordinary text color, APP-6.1) and **Savings** (green, red when negative), in whole dollars, with the savings rate under Savings as "27% of income" (savings as a percentage of income, 0% when there is no income), in muted text. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.5 | **Income**, **Expenses** and **Savings** sections, each headed by its total in whole dollars, shall list amounts, to the cent, by category (icon and name) or by category group (name only), largest amount first, in green for income and savings and the ordinary text color for expenses (APP-6.1). The page header's ⋮ menu offers **By category** and **By group**, with a check on the current one (By category when the screen opens), and applies to every section. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.6 | Selecting a row shall open the Transactions screen pre-filtered to that category (or group, or Uncategorized for an uncategorized row, TXN-1.11) and to the period's first and last day (TXN-1.7); for example Q2 2026 opens with start 2026-04-01 and end 2026-06-30. Rows highlight on hover. The filtered list includes the refunds that were netted into the row's total (CASH open questions). | Implemented |
| CASH-1.7 | An empty section shall say "No income in this period.", "No expenses in this period." or "No savings in this period." | Implemented |
| CASH-1.8 | Once any category has a necessity (CAT-3.1), the **Category** view shall list the Expenses section's categories under a heading for each of **Essential**, **Discretionary** and **Unclassified** (CAT-2.3) that has rows, with that part's share of total expenses and its total in whole dollars ("Essential · 92%  $2,000"), largest first within each. Income, and the Group view, have no such headings. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.9 | A category whose net for the period runs against its type (an Expense category whose refunds exceed its spending, or an Income category that's net money out) shall be shown as a negative amount in its section (for example "-$20.00" under Expenses) and reduce that section's total. The same applies to a group in Group view. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.10 | In every section, a category with budget streams (BUD) shall show its budget for the period and how the actual compares, in whole dollars: "$40 under", "$25 over" or "on budget". The period's budget is the streams' monthly total times the months in the period (1, 3 or 12), compared with the whole period even while it's still under way. Over budget on an expense, or under on income or savings, is shown in red. A budgeted category with nothing in the period still shows, at $0.00, so the rows add up to the budget. In Group view a group's budget is the sum of its categories'. The **Budget** and **Difference** columns (with **Actual**) appear only when something is budgeted; on narrow screens (APP-7.1) they go under the name, "Budget $500 · $40 under". | Implemented |
| CASH-1.11 | The summary card (CASH-1.4) shall show the period's budget under **Income**, **Expenses** and **Savings** ("of $8,200 budgeted") when any streams of that type exist, and under **Left over** the budgeted income less budgeted expenses and savings, Budget's Left over, when any streams exist. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.12 | The period switch's **Average** (CASH-1.1) shall show the average month over the past 12 full months before the current one, or the past 3 or 6 chosen in place of the period, starting no earlier than the month of the oldest transaction Spending counts (CASH-1.2), so two months of history average over two. The period pill shall read "Past 12 months" ("Past 2 months", "Past month" with a shorter history; on phones "12 months") and open a menu of **Past 3 months**, **Past 6 months** and **Past 12 months** with a check on the current one; its accessible name gives the window ("Average month, May 2025 to Apr 2026"). The choice lasts until the screen is left. Every figure (CASH-1.4 to CASH-1.11) shall be that window's total divided by its months, to the cent, and budgets the streams' monthly totals, so each category's average month stands next to its monthly budget. Selecting a row opens Transactions for the whole window (CASH-1.6). Spending opens on the average month when its URL has `?period=average`, so other screens can link to it. | Implemented |

## Notes

- **Savings come from everyday accounts.** Measuring what left checking, cash and credit cards works whether or not the savings account's side of a move is tracked, and matches Left over to what stayed in those accounts. Money that never passes through them, such as a 401(k) contribution taken from pay before it's deposited, isn't counted; nor is the pay it came from.
- **Debt paydown.** A regular loan payment is one expense, even though part of it reduces the loan. An extra principal payment can be split off (TXN-4) into a Transfer category such as *Extra Loan Payment* (CAT-6.2), which then counts as savings rather than spending.
- **Debug mode.** In development builds "current month" is May 2026 (APP-4.1).
- **Amount filter on the Transactions screen** compares signed values (TXN known gaps), which affects how a Spending drill-down feels only when the user adds their own filters.
