# Spending (`CASH`)

Spending shows what actually came in and went out over a calendar period, or in an average month over the past year, worked out from transactions: total income, total expenses, what was saved and at what rate, with a breakdown by category or by category group. It's the other side of the Budget (BUD): the budget is the plan, Spending is what happened.

Screen: **Spending** (`/spending`). It was called Cashflow until 2026-09-28; `/cashflow` still redirects here, and its requirements keep the `CASH` prefix.

## CASH-1 The user can see where their money came from and went

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| CASH-1.1 | The user can choose a month, a quarter or a year to view. A row under the page header shall show the period ("May 2026", "Q2 2026" or "2026"; on phones the month is shortened, "Sep 2026") between **Previous** and **Next** buttons that move one period of the same kind, each disabled where it would leave the years on offer: every year from the oldest transaction's to the newest's, and always the current year. Selecting the period opens a picker, a popover below it on wider screens and a bottom sheet on phones, with a **Month** / **Quarter** / **Year** switch, a year stepper for months and quarters, and a grid of the months, quarters or years to choose from, the one on screen marked. Choosing one shows it and closes the picker; Escape or clicking outside closes it without a change. Spending shall open on the current month. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.2 | The figures shall be built from the sum of transaction amounts per category for every transaction dated within the chosen period (both ends included), counting each part of a split transaction under its own category (TXN-4.6). Transactions in a hidden account (ACCT-1.9), and hidden transactions (TXN-1.8), shall be left out. Transactions in closed accounts (ACCT-6) count. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.3 | Amounts in categories of Income groups shall count as **income**, amounts in categories of Transfer groups shall be ignored, and amounts in categories of Expense groups shall count as **expenses**. Uncategorized amounts (CAT-5) shall count by sign: money in as income, money out as expenses. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.4 | A summary card shall lead with **Saved**: income minus expenses in whole dollars, green when zero or more, red with a "-" when negative ("-$3,734"). Beside it, the savings rate as "27% of income" (savings as a percentage of income, 0% when there is no income), in muted text. Below, **Income** (green) and **Expenses** (as a positive number, in the ordinary text color, APP-6.1), in whole dollars. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.5 | An **Income** section and an **Expenses** section, each headed by its total in whole dollars, shall list amounts, to the cent, by category (icon and name) or by category group (name only), largest amount first, in green for income and the ordinary text color for expenses (APP-6.1). One **Category** / **Group** switch, between the summary card and the Income section (full width on phones), applies to both sections. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.6 | Selecting a row shall open the Transactions screen pre-filtered to that category (or group, or Uncategorized for an uncategorized row, TXN-1.11) and to the period's first and last day (TXN-1.7); for example Q2 2026 opens with start 2026-04-01 and end 2026-06-30. Rows highlight on hover. The filtered list includes the refunds that were netted into the row's total (CASH open questions). | Implemented |
| CASH-1.7 | An empty section shall say "No income in this period." or "No expenses in this period." | Implemented |
| CASH-1.8 | Once any category has a necessity (CAT-3.1), the **Category** view shall list the Expenses section's categories under a heading for each of **Essential**, **Discretionary** and **Unclassified** (CAT-2.3) that has rows, with that part's share of total expenses and its total in whole dollars ("Essential · 92%  $2,000"), largest first within each. Income, and the Group view, have no such headings. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.9 | A category whose net for the period runs against its type (an Expense category whose refunds exceed its spending, or an Income category that's net money out) shall be shown as a negative amount in its section (for example "-$20.00" under Expenses) and reduce that section's total. The same applies to a group in Group view. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.10 | In the Income and Expenses sections, a category with budget streams (BUD) shall show its budget for the period and how the actual compares, in whole dollars: "$40 under", "$25 over" or "on budget". The period's budget is the streams' monthly total times the months in the period (1, 3 or 12), compared with the whole period even while it's still under way. Over budget on an expense, or under on income, is shown in red. A budgeted category with nothing in the period still shows, at $0.00, so the rows add up to the budget. In Group view a group's budget is the sum of its categories'. The **Budget** and **Difference** columns (with **Actual**) appear only when something is budgeted; on narrow screens (APP-7.1) they go under the name, "Budget $500 · $40 under". | Implemented |
| CASH-1.11 | The summary card (CASH-1.4) shall show the period's budget under **Income** and **Expenses** ("of $8,200 budgeted") when any streams of that type exist, and under **Saved** the budgeted income less budgeted expenses when any Income or Expense streams exist. | Implemented |
| CASH-1.12 | The period picker's switch (CASH-1.1) shall also offer **Average**, with one choice, **Past 12 months**: the average month over the full months before the current one, at most 12, starting no earlier than the month of the oldest transaction Spending counts (CASH-1.2), so three months of history average over three. The row under the page header shall read "Average month, May 2025 to Apr 2026" (on phones "Avg, May 2025 to Apr 2026"; one month is named alone, "Average month, Apr 2026", and with no history it reads "Average month"), with **Previous** and **Next** disabled. Every figure (CASH-1.4 to CASH-1.11) shall be that window's total divided by its months, to the cent, and budgets the streams' monthly totals, so each category's average month stands next to its monthly budget. Selecting a row opens Transactions for the whole window (CASH-1.6). Spending opens on the average month when its URL has `?period=average`, so other screens can link to it. | Implemented |

## Notes

- **Savings budgets aren't compared here.** Spending ignores Transfer categories (CASH-1.3), so Savings streams (BUD-1.2) only appear on the Budget screen.
- **Debt paydown.** A regular loan payment is one expense, even though part of it reduces the loan. An extra principal payment can be split off (TXN-4) into a Transfer category such as *Extra Loan Payment* (CAT-6.2), so it doesn't count as spending.
- **Debug mode.** In development builds "current month" is May 2026 (APP-4.1).
- **Amount filter on the Transactions screen** compares signed values (TXN known gaps), which affects how a Spending drill-down feels only when the user adds their own filters.
