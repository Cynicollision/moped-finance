# Transactions (`TXN`)

A transaction is one money movement on an account: a date, an amount (negative for money out, positive for money in), a merchant, and a category. Transactions feed Spending (CASH). On a manual account a transaction can also **adjust the balance** (ACCT-5); otherwise balances come only from balance history.

Screens: **Transactions** (`/transactions`), **Add Transaction**, **View Transaction**, **Edit Transaction**, **Import Transactions**. Account Details also shows recent transactions (ACCT-2.7).

## TXN-1 The user can find and review transactions

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| TXN-1.1 | The Transactions screen shall list transactions (APP-7.1) with columns, in this order, **Date**, **Account**, **Merchant**, **Category** (icon and name) and **Amount**. The amount shall follow APP-6.2: money in as "+$1,250.00" in green, money out as "$45.12" with no sign in the ordinary text color. Selecting a row opens that transaction (TXN-2.2). | Implemented |
| TXN-1.2 | The default order shall be newest first (Date, descending arrow). The user can sort by the **Date**, **Account**, **Merchant**, **Category** and **Amount** column headings, or on narrow screens the sort menu (APP-7.2). Account and Category sort by name, ignoring case, with uncategorized transactions last in ascending order. Ties sort newest first. Choosing Date first sorts newest first; choosing Merchant first sorts A to Z; choosing Amount first sorts by the signed amount ascending, so the largest outflows come first. | Implemented |
| TXN-1.3 | The list shall load 25 transactions at a time, and a **Load More** button shall append the next 25 while more exist. | Implemented |
| TXN-1.4 | When nothing matches, the screen shall say "No transactions found". | Implemented |
| TXN-1.5 | A **Filters** panel, collapsed until opened, shall let the user narrow the list by: **Min Amount**, **Max Amount**, **Direction** (TXN-1.14), **Start Date**, **End Date**, **Group**, **Category**, **Account** (a select) and **Merchant**. Filters combine (all must match). **Apply Filters** runs the search and **Clear Filters** resets it. The panel's header says how many filters are applied ("2 active"). On phones the panel is replaced by a **Filters** button in the page header, badged with that count, which opens the filters in a bottom sheet; applying or clearing closes it. | Implemented |
| TXN-1.10 | The **Start Date** and **End Date** filters shall start out empty, and **Clear Filters** empties them. Transactions can't be dated after today (APP-8.1), so there's nothing for a default End Date to hide. | Implemented |
| TXN-1.6 | The merchant filter shall match any merchant *containing* the text, ignoring case, and suggest merchants the user has used before. The category and group filters shall suggest matching names as the user types. | Implemented |
| TXN-1.7 | The screen shall accept pre-set filters from the link that opened it: account, category, group, start date and end date. Account Details' **Go To Transactions** and every row of Spending (CASH-1.6) use this. | Implemented |
| TXN-1.8 | Transactions marked **hidden** shall be left out of everything by default: the list and search (unless shown with TXN-1.13), Account Details' recent transactions, Spending (CASH-1.2), and balance adjustments (TXN-2.10). Exports still include them, with `IsHidden` (TXN-3.1). | Implemented |
| TXN-1.9 | On narrow screens (APP-7.1) the date and category shall appear under the merchant, and the account shall be left out. | Implemented |
| TXN-1.11 | Every transaction shall appear in the list and search, including Uncategorized ones (CAT-5), which show "Uncategorized". The **Category** filter shall offer Uncategorized like any other category, and Spending's uncategorized rows open the list filtered to it (CASH-1.6). | Implemented |
| TXN-1.12 | Transactions in hidden accounts (ACCT-1.9) shall be left out of the list and search, and hidden accounts left out of the **Account** filter, unless the screen was opened filtered to that account (from its Account Details' **Go To Transactions**). Transactions in closed accounts (ACCT-6) are listed as usual. | Planned |
| TXN-1.13 | The **Filters** panel shall have a **Show hidden transactions** checkbox, unchecked by default and reset by **Clear Filters**, and counted among the applied filters (TXN-1.5) when checked. When checked, hidden transactions are listed with the rest, dimmed, with a crossed-out eye icon. It's the only way to reach them in the app. | Implemented |
| TXN-1.14 | **Min Amount** and **Max Amount** shall compare the amount's size, ignoring its sign, and a **Direction** filter (**Any**, the default, **Money out**, **Money in**) shall narrow by sign, so "Min Amount 100, Money out" finds purchases of $100 or more. The server refuses a negative Min or Max Amount (APP-5.7). | Implemented |

## TXN-2 The user can record and correct transactions by hand

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| TXN-2.1 | **Add Transaction** (from the Transactions menu or an account's menu) shall show a form with: **Amount** (TXN-2.13), **Account** (required; a select of the user's open, unhidden accounts, sorted by name, plus the transaction's current account when editing; pre-selected when opened from an account), **Merchant** (text, at most 255 characters), **Original Statement** (text), **Category** (searchable by name), **Date** (date picker, required, defaults to today, not after today, APP-8.1), **Notes**, and a **Hide Transaction** checkbox. | Partial |
| TXN-2.2 | **View Transaction** (titled "Transaction Details", below Transactions, APP-1.8) shall show, read-only and in this order: **Amount** (large, signed and colored as in TXN-1.1), **Account** (a link to Account Details), **Merchant**, **Original Statement**, **Category** (icon and name), **Date**, **Notes** ("-" when empty) and **Hidden** ("Yes" or "No"). A menu offers **Edit** and **Delete**. | Implemented |
| TXN-2.3 | **Edit Transaction** shall allow changing every field, including the account and amount, except as limited for synced transactions (TXN-2.12) and split ones (TXN-4.7). | Implemented |
| TXN-2.4 | The user can delete a transaction with **Delete** in the Transaction Details menu, after confirming in a "Delete Transaction" dialog ("Delete this transaction? This can't be undone.", **Cancel** / **Delete**). Deleting says "Transaction deleted" and returns to Transactions. | Implemented |
| TXN-2.5 | The server shall reject a new transaction for an account that doesn't exist ("Account not found."), or without a merchant. | Implemented |
| TXN-2.6 | The form shall mark its required fields, **Amount**, **Account**, **Merchant** and **Date**, with an asterisk, and say under a field the user has left missing or invalid what's wrong ("Merchant is required", "Amount must be more than 0"). **Save** stays disabled until every field is valid. | Implemented |
| TXN-2.7 | The **Category** field shall start empty, showing "Uncategorized" as its placeholder, and a transaction saved with it empty is Uncategorized (CAT-5.2). It shall offer Uncategorized and the active categories (CAT-4.1), plus the transaction's current category when editing (CAT-4.2). | Implemented |
| TXN-2.8 | For a manual account (ACCT-4.3), Add and Edit Transaction shall show an **Adjust balance** checkbox, checked by default on Add. Saving with it checked changes the account's balance history as in ACCT-5.2 and ACCT-5.3. The checkbox isn't shown for a connected account. | Planned |
| TXN-2.9 | View Transaction shall show **Adjusts balance** ("Yes" or "No") for a transaction in a manual account. | Planned |
| TXN-2.10 | A hidden transaction shall not adjust its account's balance. Hiding a transaction that adjusts the balance undoes its effect, and un-hiding it applies it again (ACCT-5.3). | Planned |
| TXN-2.11 | The user can un-hide a transaction by opening it (from the list with TXN-1.13) and clearing **Hide Transaction** in Edit Transaction. | Implemented |
| TXN-2.12 | For a transaction added by bank sync, **Date**, **Amount**, **Account** and **Original Statement** shall be read-only, in the form and the API; **Merchant**, **Category**, **Notes** and **Hide Transaction** stay editable, and it can be split (TXN-4.8). It can't be deleted, only hidden, since sync won't add it again (INST-2.12). | Planned |
| TXN-2.13 | The form's **Amount** shall be entered as a positive number (required, more than 0; blank on Add) below a **Money out** / **Money in** switch, which defaults to Money out and sets the stored sign (APP-6.3). Edit Transaction shows the stored amount's size, with the switch set by its sign. The switch only sets the sign: how Spending counts the transaction comes from its category (CASH-1.3), so a refund is Money in under an Expense category. | Implemented |

## TXN-3 The user can move transactions in and out as CSV

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| TXN-3.1 | **Download Transactions** (Transactions menu, or an account's menu) shall produce a CSV named `Transactions_<timestamp>.csv`, or `Transactions_<account name>_<timestamp>.csv` for one account, with the header `Date,AccountName,Merchant,CategoryName,Amount,OriginalStatement,Notes,IsHidden`, oldest first. Dates shall be written as YYYY-MM-DD and amounts with two decimals, so the file re-imports unchanged. Split transactions are written as in TXN-4.9. It shall include hidden transactions and shall have only the header row when there are none. | Partial |
| TXN-3.2 | **Import Transactions** shall accept a CSV with all eight columns from TXN-3.1 present (`Id` is not needed). A file missing any column shall be refused with "Missing required CSV headers: …" listing them (for example "OriginalStatement, IsHidden"). A file that can't be parsed or has no data rows shall also be refused. | Implemented |
| TXN-3.3 | The user chooses a CSV file with **Choose CSV File**, then **Validate Import**, which checks it without saving ("dry run"). When the file is clean, a **Confirm Import** panel says "Please review the validated results before finalizing the import.", shows "Transactions to create: N", "Uncategorized: N" (TXN-3.4) and "Rows with errors: 0", and offers **Begin Import** and **Cancel**. The user can confirm only when the dry run found no errors. Confirming shall show "Successfully created N transactions." | Implemented |
| TXN-3.7 | When validation finds errors, the screen shall show the server's summary ("Found errors in 2 of 5 rows.") under a "Validation Error:" heading and list each row's message below it so the user can fix the file (for example "Row 2: Amount 'abc' is not a number." and "Row 3: Account name 'Nope Account' not found.", counting the header as row 1), and shall not offer **Begin Import**. | Implemented |
| TXN-3.8 | The Import Transactions screen shall list the columns the file must have: "Date, AccountName, Merchant, CategoryName, Amount, OriginalStatement, Notes, IsHidden" (TXN-3.2). | Implemented |
| TXN-3.4 | Each row's account shall be found by its **AccountName**, and its category by **CategoryName**, both ignoring case. A row whose account isn't found is an error ("Row 7: Account name 'X' not found."). A row whose CategoryName is blank, "Uncategorized", or not one of the user's categories is imported as Uncategorized (CAT-5.2). The confirm panel shall count them (TXN-3.3) and, under "Categories not found:", list each name that matched no category with the rows that use it ("'Groceriez': rows 4, 9", the first ten rows and "and N more"), so a typo can be fixed before importing. | Implemented |
| TXN-3.5 | When the import is started from an account (Account Details), every row shall go to that account regardless of its AccountName. An account that doesn't exist shall be refused ("Account not found."). | Implemented |
| TXN-3.6 | A blank Merchant is allowed by import. | Implemented |
| TXN-3.9 | An import shall be all-or-nothing. If any row has an error, nothing is saved: a confirmed import of such a file shall be refused with "Nothing was imported. Found errors in N of M rows." and the row messages. The rows are saved in one database transaction, so a failure part way through saves none of them. | Implemented |
| TXN-3.10 | Each row shall be checked, and every problem in a row reported in one message ("Row 4: Date '2026-02-30' is not a valid date. Amount 'abc' is not a number."). **Date** is required and must be a real calendar date written as YYYY-MM-DD, M/D/YYYY, or an ISO timestamp (its date part is used), and not after today (APP-8.1). **Amount** is required: a number with an optional sign, `$` and thousands separators, at most two decimal places, and at most 9,999,999,999.99 either way. **IsHidden** must be true, false (any case) or blank (false). **Merchant** is at most 255 characters. A row with the wrong number of columns is an error. | Implemented |
| TXN-3.11 | When the import targets a manual account (TXN-3.5), the screen shall offer **Adjust balances**, unchecked by default. With it checked, each imported transaction adjusts the balance as in ACCT-5.2, in the same database transaction as the import (TXN-3.9). Without it, imported transactions don't adjust balances, so a history imported alongside its balance history isn't counted twice (ACCT-3.11). | Planned |

## TXN-4 The user can split a transaction across categories

A split divides one transaction into **parts**, each with its own amount and category, for example a store receipt covering groceries and household supplies, or a loan payment with extra principal. The transaction itself (account, date, total amount, original statement) doesn't change; the parts decide how it's categorized.

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| TXN-4.1 | The user can split any transaction that isn't already split, with **Split** on View Transaction's menu, into 2 to 20 parts. | Planned |
| TXN-4.2 | Each part shall have an amount (entered as in TXN-2.13, with the switch defaulting to the transaction's direction), a category, a merchant (defaulting to the transaction's) and notes. The parts' amounts shall add up exactly to the transaction's amount; the split dialog shows "Remaining: *amount*" and keeps **Save** disabled until it's $0.00. | Planned |
| TXN-4.3 | The split dialog shall open with two parts. The last part's amount shall fill itself with the remainder as the user types the others, until the user edits it. **Add part** adds a new last part that takes over the remainder, so splitting in two needs only the first amount. | Planned |
| TXN-4.4 | The Transactions list shall show each part as its own row, with the transaction's date and account, the part's merchant, category and amount, and a split icon. Filters and sorting apply to parts, so a category filter (and a Spending drill-down, CASH-1.6) finds just the matching part. Selecting a part opens the transaction. | Planned |
| TXN-4.5 | View Transaction shall show a split transaction's total and list its parts. **Edit split** reopens the split dialog; **Unsplit** (after confirming) turns it back into one transaction with the first part's category, merchant and notes. | Planned |
| TXN-4.6 | Spending (CASH-1.2) and budget averages (BUD-1.8) shall count each part under its own category. | Planned |
| TXN-4.7 | A split transaction's amount, date and account can't be changed while it's split; Unsplit first. Hiding it hides all its parts, and it adjusts the balance (ACCT-5) once, by its total. | Planned |
| TXN-4.8 | A synced transaction (TXN-2.12) can be split like any other; its parts belong to the user and sync doesn't change them. | Planned |
| TXN-4.9 | **Download Transactions** shall write each part as its own row, with the transaction's date, account, original statement and hidden flag, and a `SplitId` column that's the same for every part of one transaction (blank otherwise). Import shall accept the column as optional and rebuild the split from rows sharing a `SplitId`, refusing a group whose rows don't share a date, account, original statement and hidden flag. | Planned |

## Known gaps

- **Every account is offered in the form (TXN-2.1),** in creation order, hidden ones included.
- **Splits don't exist (TXN-4).** The `is_split` and `split_parent_id` columns are there, and import writes `false` and blank to them, but nothing reads them.
- **No duplicate detection on import.** Importing the same file twice creates every transaction twice.
